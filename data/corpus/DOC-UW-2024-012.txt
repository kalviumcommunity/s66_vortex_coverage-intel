SECTION - ISO CONSTRUCTION CLASSES 1 THROUGH 6
Class 1: Frame — Buildings where the exterior walls, floors and roof are constructed of wood or other combustible materials.
Class 2: Joisted Masonry — Buildings where the exterior walls are constructed of masonry materials such as brick, concrete block, or stone, but floors and roof are combustible wood.
Class 3: Non-Combustible — Buildings where exterior walls, floors and roof are constructed of and supported by metal, asbestos, gypsum or other non-combustible materials.
Class 4: Masonry Non-Combustible — Exterior walls constructed of masonry or fire-resistive materials not less than 4 inches thick with non-combustible floors and roofs.
Class 5: Modified Fire Resistive — Buildings with exterior walls, floors and roof of masonry or other fire-resistive materials with fire-resistance rating of at least 1 hour but less than 2 hours.
Class 6: Fire Resistive — Buildings with exterior bearing walls of solid masonry or reinforced concrete not less than 4 inches thick and floors/roofs of reinforced concrete with a fire-resistance rating of at least 2 hours.
