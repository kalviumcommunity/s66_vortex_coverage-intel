SECTION - WATER DAMAGE INVESTIGATION GUIDELINES
1. VERIFICATION OF FREEZING TEMPERATURE MAINTENANCE
When investigating frozen pipe ruptures under HO-3 Peril 14, claims adjusters must verify that the insured maintained building heat at or above 55 degrees Fahrenheit prior to the freezing event.
The adjuster should document:
a. Smart thermostat logs or utility heating consumption records for the 72 hours preceding loss.
b. If heat was not maintained, whether the domestic water supply was completely shut off and plumbing lines drained.
c. If neither condition was met, coverage must be flagged for potential exclusion under neglect and failure to maintain heat.

2. GRADUAL SEEPAGE VS SUDDEN DISCHARGE
Leakage occurring continuously or repeatedly over 14 days or more constitutes gradual seepage and is strictly excluded under Section I - Perils Insured Against, Item 12.b(2). Sub-surface water line tests and moisture meter readings should establish the onset timeline.
