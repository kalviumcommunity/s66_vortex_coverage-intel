AGREEMENT & DEFINITIONS
We agree to provide the insurance described in this policy in return for the premium and compliance with all applicable policy provisions for policies issued in calendar year 2023.

SECTION I - PROPERTY COVERAGES
COVERAGE A — DWELLING
We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling.

COVERAGE B — OTHER STRUCTURES
We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space.

SECTION I - PERILS INSURED AGAINST
12. ACCIDENTAL DISCHARGE OR OVERFLOW OF WATER OR STEAM
a. Under this 2023 edition, accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam is covered from plumbing systems, subject to a 72-hour discovery notice requirement.
b. Freezing coverage requires the temperature of the premises to have been maintained at or above 50 degrees Fahrenheit. (Superseded by 2024 edition which requires 55 degrees Fahrenheit).
c. Sump pump failure is strictly excluded unless the water backup endorsement is attached.
