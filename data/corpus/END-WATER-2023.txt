SECTION - ENDORSEMENT PROVISIONS
SCHEDULE OF COVERAGE
Limit of Liability: $10,000 per occurrence.
Deductible: $500 per loss.

1. COVERAGE MODIFICATION
We insure for direct physical loss, not caused by the negligence of any insured, to property covered under Section I caused by:
(1) Water or water-borne material which backs up through sewers or drains; or
(2) Water or water-borne material which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump or related equipment, even if the overflow or discharge results from mechanical breakdown of the sump pump.
This coverage does not apply to direct physical loss resulting from power interruption unless the power failure was caused by a covered Peril on the residence premises.

2. PRECEDENCE & RELATION TO BASE POLICY
This endorsement overrides and supersedes Section I - Exclusion 1.b (Water Damage Exclusion - sewer backup and sump discharge) of policy POL-HO3-2024. All other terms, limits, and exclusions of the base policy remain in full effect.
